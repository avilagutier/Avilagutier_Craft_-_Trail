Bergen welcomed us with its trademark sea mist parting over the lush forested hills of Mount Fløyen.

Walking among the colorful wooden houses of Bryggen feels like stepping into the Hanseatic League's prime, with a contemporary twist: hidden taprooms behind timber beams pouring Nordic Farmhouse Ales fermented at warm temperatures with local farmhouse Kveik cultures.

### Highlights
- **Fisketorget Seafood Market**: Fresh King crab and langoustines overlooking timber boats, paired with local Hardanger apple ciders.
- **Mount Ulriken Ridge Hike**: The highest of Bergen's seven peaks, rewarding with expansive panoramas of the North Sea coastline.
- **Nordic Microbreweries**: Wild berry sours infused with freshly foraged Norwegian juniper branches.

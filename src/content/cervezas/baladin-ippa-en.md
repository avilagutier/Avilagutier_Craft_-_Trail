Visiting Rome (late May to early June) — I love this city. It was a unique experience traveling solo, yet thrilling to wander around and discover new spots.

A friend recommended **Open Baladin Rome**; the sheer variety of craft beers they carry is truly impressive, and the decor is spectacular.

### Technical Profile & Tasting Notes
- **Style:** Italian IPA (100% Italian-grown ingredients)
- **ABV:** 5.5% vol.
- **Bitterness (IBU):** 31-33
- **Color (EBC):** 23-25 (bright amber)
- **Serving Temperature:** 6-8 °C

Poured with a lively head, releasing fresh aromas of tangerine, grapefruit zest, and delicate herbal notes from local hops. A very approachable, crisp bitterness that made it a perfect refreshing pint in Rome.

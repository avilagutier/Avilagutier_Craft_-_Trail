Sampled at **Open Baladin** during my solo trip to Rome. An outstanding guest tap pick representing top independent Italian craft brewing.

### Technical Profile & Tasting Notes
- **Style:** DDH Session NEIPA
- **ABV:** 4.0% vol.
- **Fermentation:** Top-fermenting
- **Color:** Light golden, hazy and juicy
- **Serving Temperature:** 5-8 °C

Remarkably low ABV with huge hop character thanks to the Double Dry Hopping. Bursting with ripe mango, passionfruit, and subtle citrus zest. Silky mouthfeel with minimal bitterness, dangerously drinkable.

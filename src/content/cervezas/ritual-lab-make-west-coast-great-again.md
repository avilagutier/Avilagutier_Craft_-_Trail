One of my favorite neighborhoods in Rome is **Trastevere**, and this time I visited the legendary [**Ma Che Siete Venuti A Fà**](https://www.instagram.com/machesietevenutiafa/) located at *Via Benedetta, 25*.

The vibrant atmosphere with everyone hanging outside on the cobblestone alley enjoying craft beers was one of the best experiences in the city — meeting fellow beer enthusiasts, sharing conversations, and soaking in authentic Roman pub culture.

### Tasting Notes & Profile
Brewed by the award-winning Italian microbrewery [**Ritual Lab**](https://www.ritual-lab.it/en):
- **Style:** West Coast IPA (Cryo Hops)
- **ABV:** 6.2% vol.
- **Appearance:** Slightly hazy golden color with a dense, persistent white head
- **Aroma & Flavor:** Sharp piney and resinous character on the palate, balanced by vibrant citrus notes (grapefruit zest, tangerine) from advanced Cryo hop dosing. 

Crisp, assertive bitterness that lingers pleasantly without being astringent. A stellar interpretation of the West Coast style crafted in Italy.

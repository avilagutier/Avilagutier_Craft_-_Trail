A very special pour at [**Ma Che Siete Venuti A Fà**](https://www.instagram.com/machesietevenutiafa/) in Trastevere (*Via Benedetta, 25*): **Benedikta 25**, an exclusive Italian Grape Ale / Sour collaboration brewed with the world-revered [**Brasserie Cantillon**](https://www.cantillon.be/?lang=fr) to celebrate the 25th anniversary of the pub.

### Tasting Notes & Profile
- **Brewery Collaboration:** Brasserie Cantillon (Belgium) × Ma Che Siete Venuti A Fà (Rome)
- **Style:** Italian Grape Ale / Wild Lambic Sour
- **Occasion:** 25th Anniversary Celebration
- **Appearance:** Deep reddish-amber hue with gentle carbonation
- **Flavor & Mouthfeel:** Light and lively on the palate at first, showcasing the innovative Italian philosophy of marrying winemaking grape must with spontaneous fermentation. It develops into an intensely tart, complex funk with a deeply dry, bitter finish in the aftertaste.

A rare and fascinating beer that pushes style boundaries.

Another house pour tasted at **Brasserie Neptune** (*6 Rue Bressieux, Grenoble*). **Vesna** is their seasonal spring amber ale ("Vesna" meaning spring in Slavic mythology).

### Tasting Notes & Profile
- **Style:** Spring Amber Ale
- **ABV:** ~5.5% vol.
- **Tasting Notes:** Clear coppery amber hue with ivory foam. Delicate toasty malt profile featuring caramel, fresh toasted bread, and subtle hazelnut, balanced by gentle herbal and floral hops. A smooth, comforting pint after a long day of synchrotron measurements.

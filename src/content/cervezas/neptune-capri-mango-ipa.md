During my research stay in Grenoble, I visited [**Brasserie Neptune**](https://brasserietaproomneptune.fr/) located at *6 Rue Bressieux*. 

Neptune is an outstanding brewpub to have dinner and taste craft beer made right in Grenoble with pure passion. Their taps rotate weekly with fresh house creations.

### Tasting Notes & Profile
- **Style:** Fruited IPA with mango and citrus
- **ABV:** ~6.0% vol.
- **Tasting Notes:** Pours with a hazy golden body and a tight white foam. Explosive bouquet of ripe mango, passionfruit, and sweet grapefruit. The mouthfeel is soft and juicy, supported by a restrained yet clean hop bitterness that leaves the fruit character in full spotlight.

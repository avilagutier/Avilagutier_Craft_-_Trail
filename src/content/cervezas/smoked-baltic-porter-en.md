Tasted on a drizzly autumn evening in Edinburgh, sitting right by the stone fireplace at *The Highland Cask*.

Bottom-fermented with lager yeast at cold temperatures and conditioned for four months in casks previously housing Islay single malt whisky. The peat smoke enhances without overpowering the roasted barley and subtle vanilla layers.

### Sensations
Warm, comforting, and velvet-like body. Thick mocha-colored head with micro-fine lacing. Undoubtedly one of the finest porters sampled in Europe.

Located at *4 Place Sainte-Claire* in the bustling heart of Grenoble, [**Not A Beer**](https://notabeer.fr/) is one of the premier craft beer sanctuaries in the city. 

A fantastic spot to drop in with friends to unwind and share a stellar afternoon over rare independent beers.

### Tap Lineup & Experience
While Not A Beer showcases cutting-edge craft beers from all corners of France and international craft bastions, their focus on local and regional microbreweries from the Auvergne-Rhône-Alpes region is exceptional:
- **Tasting Profile:** Poured a selection of house-selected regional IPAs highlighting bold citrus zest (grapefruit, lemon peel) and deep resinous, piney mountain hops.
- **Atmosphere:** Lively, friendly, and unpretentious — a must-visit craft beer haven right across from the historic Sainte-Claire covered market.

A craft brewing masterpiece served straight from the draft line in the historic Barrio de las Letras in Madrid.

This Double IPA features a massive double dry-hopping charge of Citra, Mosaic, and Simcoe. Perfect haziness, luminous golden-orange color, and a dense, creamy white foam head with remarkable retention.

### Tasting Experience
The 8.2% ABV is so seamlessly integrated that it drinks astonishingly smooth. Perfect after an afternoon trail walk or while planning upcoming mountain expeditions.

The third beer tasted at **Brasserie Neptune** (*6 Rue Bressieux, Grenoble*): **Ishtar**, a bold and commanding Triple Amber Ale named after the ancient Mesopotamian goddess.

### Tasting Notes & Profile
- **Style:** Triple Amber Ale
- **ABV:** ~8.2% vol.
- **Tasting Notes:** Deep ruby-amber color. Aromas of candied orange peel, raisins, Belgian candi sugar, and warm clove-like yeast esters. Rich, round, and warming on the palate, hiding its strength behind an impeccable malt sweetness and a dry, lightly spicy finish. A standout brew from their lineup.

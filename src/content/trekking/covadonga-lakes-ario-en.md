Departing from the Buferrera parking lot near the Covadonga Lakes, skirting along the southern shoreline of Lake Ercina among grazing Casina cattle and lush high alpine meadows.

The trail climbs steadily through the rocky Canaleta del Casetón into the Majada de las Bobias, where mountain shepherds still produce the legendary smoked Gamonéu del Puerto cheese in traditional stone shelters.

### The Reward at the Mountain Hut
Reaching the Vega de Ario refuge rewards hikers with an extraordinary vantage point: an unobstructed aerial perspective plunging down into the depths of the Cares Gorge, with Torrecerredo peak dominating the horizon.

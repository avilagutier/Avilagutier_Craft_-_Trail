One of the defining classic mountaineering routes of the entire Pyrenean range. Setting out from the Ordesa Meadow at first light with morning dew blanketing the ancient beech forests.

The trail winds along the Arazas river, passing cascading falls (Arripas, La Cueva, and El Estrecho) before opening into the majestic glacial cirque of Cola de Caballo. From there, tackling the Soaso chains leads into high-altitude karst limestone terrain up to Refugio de Góriz.

### Trail Specs & Tips
- **Trailhead**: Pradera de Ordesa (Torla, Huesca).
- **Elevation change**: +1,150 m / -1,150 m.
- **Terrain**: Shaded woodland path transitioning into an exposed chain-assisted ledge (or alternative Muleteers track) and alpine scree.
- **Hydration**: Natural springs available on the lower half; filter water above 2,000 meters.
